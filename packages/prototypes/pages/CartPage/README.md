# CartPage

Responsive Storybook draft based on the PC shopping cart inspected on 2026-10-13. Uses Header/Footer, Checkbox, QuantitySelect, Drawer, Input, Button, Tabs and ProductList; cart rows stay page-owned because ProductCard is for discovery collections.

This delivery targets light mode only. The new component and cart stories fix the light theme; existing shared theme tokens and other components are unchanged.

## Demo data and rules

- Underlined action buttons (Remove, Save for later, View all, Learn more, Redeem and Edit) keep transparent hover/pressed backgrounds and a constant 1px underline in every state. They retain Button's keyboard focus ring; ordinary tertiary actions keep their background feedback.

- Both sidebar and drawer promo-code forms use `Input density="compact"`, with a native external label and a separate redeem button. The field/button are 40px on PC and 48px below 1024px; validation appears below the field without a manual button offset.
- Redeem and Edit share the MD button padding (16px per side) and responsive height (40px on PC, 48px below 1024px), with matching right edges for their labels. Width follows the localized label.

- Product imagery/identities, visible price examples, payment/guarantee artwork and shipping examples reference the observed cart. Titles are shortened and English copy is authored for the prototype.
- Recommendations use the first 32 unique products read from the live English cart's Recommended products panel on 2026-10-14: see `recommendations.snapshot.json` for source URL, English names, images, links and displayed prices. At the current four-column desktop width, these fill eight rows; narrower screens retain the responsive column count. Names and links retain the English source for both preview locales. Quantities, purchase limits, selection, fulfillment and coupons DEMO10/DEMO5 are synthetic. No customer cart is copied, and no data is persisted or sent to the cart service.
- Only selected, available merchandise contributes to the total. Price values are integer cents and displayed row prices are quantity-adjusted line totals.
- Merchant checkboxes and page-wide select-all controls stay unchecked for partial selection and become checked only when all available items in their group are selected. Clicking a checkbox selects or clears its whole group. This applies to both desktop and mobile select-all controls.
- Shipping progress remains visible at zero, partial and full completion, and follows selected item quantities.
- Each merchant qualifies free shipping from its own selected current-price subtotal before coupon deductions. No selection incurs no shipping. DEMO10 takes $10 off a merchandise subtotal of at least $100; DEMO5 takes $5 off at least $50. Both exclude shipping and become inapplicable below their thresholds. Only one coupon applies at a time. These are fixture rules, not verified production business logic.
- Tax is not estimated. Checkout opens a local order summary and does not submit an order or collect payment. Delete and Save for later use inline success placeholders; saved items can move back to the cart, recommendations add locally, and stock notifications are local demo state.
- Checkout remains red and enabled before selection, matching the observed source. A local alert appears only after an attempt with no selected items; selecting an item clears it. The protection title and explanation share a div with a 4px desktop gap and a 2px mobile gap.
- The protection drawer follows the live Chinese cart inspected on 2026-10-14: four icon headings, 12 payment marks, five payment-security certification marks, privacy copy, four shopping-protection points and three delivery points. Chinese copy and artwork come from that panel; English copy is translated for the prototype. It has no footer action. Desktop uses the observed 32px title, 72px close-control band and 24px body top padding; mobile retains the shared Drawer bottom-sheet behavior. Body line heights use design-system typography tokens.
- Protection content uses four sections with a 24px gap. Each section groups its heading and description/list with an 8px gap; payment methods and certification form separate groups 12px apart, with 4px inside the certification group and between list entries. Containers own this rhythm through `gap`, without child margin/padding spacers.
- Coupon and unavailable-item drawers follow the observed desktop layout. Mobile stacking and the fixed checkout bar are a proposed responsive composition requiring design review. The existing PC-only Footer is hidden below 1024px.
- Cart data is initialized from props; the Storybook host remounts on locale/scenario changes. Production API integration, real inventory, payment provider widgets and policy/legal copy review are outside this prototype.

## Inline action feedback — 2026-10-14

Delete and Save for later replace the affected row at its original index with a 56px-high placeholder, a 40px contained thumbnail and an 8px image/text gap. The message receives programmatic focus. Placeholder data is separate from real cart items, so counts, selection, prices, shipping and checkout never include it. There is one latest placeholder, with no expiry timer or Undo. A subsequent cart update (selection, quantity, coupon, add, delete or save) clears/replaces it; switching tabs or opening details does not. If a merchant loses its final item, its group disappears and focus falls back to the cart title. An emptied unavailable drawer keeps its existing empty-state view and receives focus there.

This is local success-state behavior. No artificial request delay, production cart mutation or failure simulation is added. Removing an item from the saved collection simply removes that entry; moving it back to the cart remains available.

The default fixture includes one available and one sold-out saved product.
