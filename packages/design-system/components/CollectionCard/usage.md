# CollectionCard — 专辑卡片

用一张卡片链接到品牌或品类专辑。传入 `item` 的标题、目标地址和商品缩略图，三张图片排成一行，四张图片排成两行两列。整张卡片共享一个链接，标题提供链接的可访问名称。

卡片默认使用浅灰色表面。单行三图卡片最小宽度为 320px，四宫格可随容器缩小。相关专辑仅有一张卡片时，它会填满内容区。Storybook 内嵌预览可设置 `item.target="_top"` 打开专辑。

# CollectionCard — Collection card

Link to a brand or category collection with a title, destination and product thumbnails in `item`. Three thumbnails form one row. Four form a two-by-two grid. The title names a single link covering the entire card.

The card uses the secondary surface. A three-image strip has a minimum width of 320px. A four-image grid can shrink with its container. A single card in the related collections rail fills the available width. Set `item.target="_top"` to navigate out of an embedded preview.
